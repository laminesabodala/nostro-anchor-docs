## What this demonstrates

An authenticated partner created a quoted payment, transferred the test asset with the required correlation memo, and received a completed payout status after the PI-SPI sandbox leg.

The Stellar and PI-SPI operations remain separate. The PI-SPI payment used prefunded virtual money; the testnet asset did not create fiat liquidity.

## What remains outside this evidence

This single run does not establish production settlement, production bank authorization, live foreign-exchange liquidity, universal bank availability, recovery under every failure condition, or duplicate-payment protection across all scenarios. Testnet explorers may also lose older history following network resets.
